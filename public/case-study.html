Project 2 — Business Automation

A lead qualification system that scores, drafts outreach, and logs every submission automatically

Built with n8n, Claude, and a self-hosted $0/month infrastructure stack — end to end, from public intake form to CRM, with a human approval step and real error handling in between.

View live dashboard Try the intake form Contact

The problem this solves

Most inbound leads lose intent within minutes of submitting a form. A human reviewing and replying to every lead by hand doesn't scale, and generic auto-replies get ignored. This system scores each lead for fit automatically, drafts a first-touch message that references what the lead actually asked for, and puts the final decision in front of a person before anything goes out.

How it works

Form submission   → validated (honeypot + email check)   → scored and drafted by Claude   → logged to Sheets   → sent to Slack for approval   → approved leads pushed to HubSpot   → dashboard reflects it all in real time

What makes it a real system, not a demo

  • Validation before cost. A honeypot field and email check filter out spam before it ever reaches a paid AI call.
  • Graceful AI failure. If the scoring call fails, the lead is still logged — flagged "Needs Review" instead of lost.
  • Human in the loop. Every AI-drafted message goes to Slack for a person to approve before it's treated as real.
  • Deduplication. Approved leads upsert into the CRM by email, so a repeat submission updates rather than duplicates.
  • Retries and alerts. External calls retry automatically on failure, and any unhandled error pings a dedicated Slack channel with the exact node that broke.
  • A visible audit trail. The dashboard shows what was processed, what was rejected, and why — not just a black box.

Stack

n8n (self-hosted) Claude API Oracle Cloud Docker + Caddy Google Sheets Slack HubSpot Next.js Vercel

Notes on the build

Why self-host n8n instead of n8n Cloud?
No per-execution limits, no monthly fee, and it's the same setup I'd recommend to a cost-conscious client — full control over the server for $0.
Why an approval step instead of fully autonomous outreach?
Businesses don't trust an AI to message prospects unsupervised. A person reviews the draft in Slack and approves it with one click — the AI recommends, a human decides.
What would change for a real client deployment?
Move from SQLite to Postgres past a certain volume, split AI scoring from outreach drafting to only pay for drafts on qualified leads, and add authentication to the dashboard.
